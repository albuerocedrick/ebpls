# eBPLS System Update: Patch Notes (Phases 1 to 6.1)

Welcome to the latest update of the eBPLS system! Based on your feedback, we have successfully rolled out several major features designed to improve transparency, automate processes, and enhance the overall user experience for both applicants and municipal staff.

Here is a summary of what's new and how to test it.

---

## 🏛️ For the Treasury Office

### 1. Digital Revenue Code (Fee Schedule)
- **What’s New**: We have fully digitized the official Revenue Code! There is a new **"Fee Schedule"** page available in the Treasury sidebar.
- **How it helps**: Treasury officers can now view all 13 categories of municipal fees (Manufacturers, Piggery, Poultry, Service Establishments, etc.) directly in the system without manually referencing paper documents.
- **Where to see it**: Log in as Treasury → Click "Fee Schedule" in the sidebar.

---

## 👨‍💼 For the Applicant

### 2. Transparent Fee Assessment Breakdown
- **What’s New**: Applicants can now see exactly what they are paying for. Once the Treasury assesses their application, a detailed "Fee Assessment Breakdown" card appears.
- **How it helps**: It clearly lists the exact Mayor's Permit Fee (based on their specific business type) and all other municipal fees (Sanitary, Fire Safety, Garbage, etc.) so there are no surprises.
- **Where to see it**: Log in as an Applicant → View an application with the status **"Assessed"** or **"For Payment"**.

### 3. Step-by-Step Procedure Guide
- **What’s New**: A new dynamic procedure guide tells the applicant exactly what they need to do next based on their current status.
- **How it helps**: Removes confusion. Whether they are "Awaiting BPLO review," need to "Pay Online/OTC," or their permit is "Ready for Download," the system guides them.
- **Where to see it**: Log in as an Applicant → Open any application → Look for the "Next Steps / Procedure" section.

### 4. Dynamic Business Activity Inputs (Tiered Fees)
- **What’s New**: When filling out Step 3 of the application, the system now automatically asks for the exact metrics needed to calculate the fee.
- **How it helps**: For example, if they select "Piggery", it will specifically ask for the "Number of Heads". If they select "Boarding House", it asks for the "Number of Boarders".

### 5. Fee Breakdown During Online Payment
- **What’s New**: When clicking "Pay via GCash/Card", a new collapsible "View Fee Breakdown" toggle is available inside the blue payment card.
- **How it helps**: Applicants can double-check their itemized bill right before entering their payment details.

---

## 🔔 Real-Time Notifications & Emails

### 6. Universal In-App Pop-Up Notifications
- **What’s New**: The applicant dashboard now features a comprehensive real-time notification system. A prominent pop-up will appear at the top of the screen whenever their application status changes!
- **Covered Statuses**:
  - 🔍 **Under Review**: "Your application is now being reviewed."
  - ✅ **Verified**: "Your documents have been verified."
  - 📋 **Assessed**: "Your fees have been assessed."
  - 💳 **Payment Confirmed**: "Your payment has been received."
  - ⚠️ **Returned**: "Your application needs corrections." (Includes BPLO remarks)
  - 📄 **Permit Ready**: "Your permit is ready for download." (Includes BPLO remarks)
- **New Feature**: Pop-ups now include a **timespan** (e.g., "2 hours ago") so applicants know exactly when the update happened.

### 7. Automated Email Updates
- **What’s New**: In addition to the pop-ups, the system now sends an official email for **every single status update** (including the newly added "Under Review" email).
- **How it helps**: Applicants don't need to stay logged in to track their progress. They will be notified via email every step of the way.

---

## 🏢 For the BPLO Office

### 8. "Notify Applicant" Button for Completed Permits
- **What’s New**: When an application is fully completed, the BPLO now has a "Notify Applicant" button in their Completed Queue.
- **How it helps**: The BPLO officer can type a custom note (e.g., "Please bring valid ID when claiming the physical copy") which immediately triggers a pop-up and email to the applicant.
- **Where to see it**: Log in as BPLO → Go to the "Completed" queue → Click "Notify Applicant".
