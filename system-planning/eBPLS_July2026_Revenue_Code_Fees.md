# Official Municipality of Liliw Revenue Code — Mayor's Permit Fees

Based on the document provided, here is the complete, categorized breakdown of the Mayor's Permit fees. These will be added to the `fee_schedule_items` table in the database and displayed on the new Treasury Fee Schedule page.

## Special Rules & Surcharges
* **Duplicate Permit**: ₱200.00 for each duplicate permit issued.
* **Late Payment Surcharge**: 25% surcharge on the original amount due if not paid within the prescribed period (January of each year for renewals).
* **Default/Catch-All Fee**: ₱500.00 for any business establishment or undertaking not specifically enumerated below.
